🕹️ Systems Guide & Drop Mechanics

Navigate your cursor right or left across the game frame to steer your paddle instantly under the ball's bouncing path.

  • Hard Bricks: Multi-layered neon panels (Pink and Yellow) that require 2 to 3 hits to smash completely.
  • Neon Power-Ups (+): Catch falling Green dots to trigger Multi-ball, Wide Paddles, Lasers (Click to fire!), or Slow Ball cooldowns.
  • Risk Power-Downs (X): Dodge falling Red dots to keep your paddle from shrinking or the ball from aggressively speeding up.
